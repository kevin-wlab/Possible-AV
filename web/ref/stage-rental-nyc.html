Stage sizes

How big a stage for your event.

Most modular stage decks are 4 ft by 8 ft, so stages grow in 4 ft steps. These are starting points: we confirm the size once we know the space, the program and where the audience sits.

8 × 8 ft

Podium or one speaker

Two decks. A lectern, a presenter and space to step aside.

8 × 16 ft

Panel or small band

Four decks. Chairs for a panel of three to five, or a duo with their gear.

12 × 16 ft

Launch or DJ set

Six decks. A presenter, a product reveal, or a DJ booth with space around it.

16 × 24 ft and up

Band or full show

Twelve decks or more. A full band, a choir, or a show with screens behind.

Stage height

High enough to see, low enough to feel close.

Every stage gets stairs, and open edges get railings when the height calls for them.

Low

Around 8 to 16 in

For a small audience, a panel on a riser, or a DJ who wants to stay level with the crowd.

Middle

Around 24 in

The usual height for a talk or a launch with seated rows.

High

Around 32 in and up

For a large standing audience, so the back of the crowd can see over the front.

Questions

Stage rental, briefly.

What size stage do I need?

Start with what happens on it. A podium and one speaker fits on 8 by 8 ft, a panel or a small band on 8 by 16 ft, and a full band on 16 by 24 ft or more. Send us the program and the headcount and we will size it.

What is a stage deck?

A flat, modular platform on legs, most often 4 ft by 8 ft. Decks lock together side by side to make a stage, a riser or a runway at the height you choose.

How high should a stage be?

Around 8 to 16 in for a small audience, around 24 in for seated rows, and 32 in or more when a large standing crowd needs to see over the people in front.

Do you set up and take down the stage?

Yes. Our crew builds the stage on site, levels it, adds stairs and skirting, and strikes it at the end.

What does stage rental cost?

It depends on the size, the height, the finishing, the access to the space and the crew hours. Send us the details and we reply with an itemized quote.

Can lighting and screens go on the stage?

Yes. Lighting, sound, screens and microphones come from the same crew, planned with the stage.

Do you rent truss on its own?

Yes. Truss and rigging can be rented for lights, screens or signs, set up by our crew.
