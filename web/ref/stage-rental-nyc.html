Questions

Stage & truss rental, briefly.

What size stage do I need?

It depends on how many people are on it and how far back the audience goes. Tell us the program and the headcount and we will size it.

Do you set up and take down the stage?

Yes. Our crew builds the stage on site and strikes it at the end.

Can lighting and screens go on the stage?

Yes. Lighting, sound, screens and microphones come from the same crew, planned with the stage.

Do you rent truss on its own?

Yes. Truss and rigging can be rented for lights, screens or signs, set up by our crew.
