Questions

Event production, briefly.

What does an event production company do?

It plans and runs the technical side of an event: staging, lighting, sound, screens, streaming and the crew. Possible AV also builds sets and interactive pieces in house.

Do you work with event planners and agencies?

Yes. We work for brands directly, and with planners and agencies who set the creative and want one team to deliver it.

Can you produce an event outside New York?

Yes. The same crew and equipment travel from Brooklyn. See touring and out-of-town AV.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can, especially for large events. Tight timelines are worth a message too: tell us the date.

Do you include LED walls?

LED walls come from our sister site, ledwall.nyc, and we plan them into the same production.
