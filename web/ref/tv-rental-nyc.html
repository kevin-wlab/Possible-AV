Questions

TV & screen rental, briefly.

Can I rent a TV for one day?

Yes. Single-day TV rentals for events and meetings are a normal job, delivered and set up.

Do you rent touch screens?

Yes. Touch screens with built-in Android or Windows, for kiosks, check-in, product catalogs and interactive pieces.

Do you load the content?

Yes. Send the files and we load and test them before the screens leave Brooklyn.

Can screens be wall mounted?

Yes. Screens go out on stands or mounted to the wall, depending on the space.

Do you sell screens as well?

Yes. We sell and install touch screens and displays for offices, stores and showrooms.
