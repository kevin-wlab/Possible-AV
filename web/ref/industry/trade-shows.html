Questions

Before you book.

What do you handle in a booth?

The whole AV side: displays, audio and interactive pieces, plus fixtures from our shop, planned as one system.

Can you work to the show's build and teardown windows?

Yes. The booth is built and tested in Brooklyn first, so the time on the floor goes into placing and checking.

Is someone on site during the show?

Yes. A technician is on the floor for opening and on call for the rest of the show.

Can you add an LED wall to the booth?

Yes. LED walls go through our sister site ledwall.nyc, and we plan them in with the rest of the booth.

How do we start?

Send us the show, the booth size and what the booth has to do. We reply within 24 hours.
