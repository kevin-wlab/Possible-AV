Questions

AV rental, briefly.

Does AV rental in NYC include setup?

Yes. Every rental goes out with our crew: delivery, setup, a technician while it runs, and strike.

Can I rent just one piece of equipment?

Yes. A single TV, projector or speaker is a normal job. It still arrives set up and tested.

Do you deliver across all five boroughs?

Yes. We deliver from Brooklyn to Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island, and to Jersey City and Long Island.

What if you do not carry something?

We source it and bring it in with everything else, on the same quote and the same schedule.

Do you rent LED walls?

LED walls are rented through our sister site, ledwall.nyc, and we plan them in with the rest of the AV.
