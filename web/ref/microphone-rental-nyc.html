Questions

Microphone rental, briefly.

How many wireless microphones do I need?

One for each voice that can be live at the same moment, plus a spare. Our wireless microphone guide walks through common programs.

Can I rent microphones without a sound system?

Yes. We can feed the microphones into the system already in your space, or bring speakers and a mixer as well.

Headset, lavalier or handheld?

Handhelds are the most forgiving, headsets keep the level steady for presenters who move, and lavaliers are the most discreet. We will suggest a mix for your program.

Is a technician included?

Yes. A technician sets up and checks every channel, and can stay to run them during the event.
