About the work

What we do.

What does Possible AV do?

AV rental, installation and production: displays, projection, audio, lighting and staging, live video, networks, interactive, fabrication, and the logistics to bring it all, done in house by one crew.

Do you own your equipment?

We own our core equipment and run it ourselves. Anything we do not carry, we source and bring in with everything else.

Do you do LED walls?

LED walls are handled by our sister site, ledwall.nyc. We handle everything around them: signal, sound, networks and playback.

Can you make content, or just bring equipment?

Both. Content, 3D and custom builds are made in house, and we play back content your team provides. If Resolume or TouchDesigner does it, we do it.

Logistics

How a job runs.

Where do you work?

We are based in Brooklyn, New York. For work outside New York, the same crew travels with the equipment.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can, especially for large or multi-city work. Tight timelines are worth a message too: tell us the date.

Do you handle load-in and strike?

Yes. Our own truck and crew deliver, set up, run the event and take everything away.

What if the venue is tricky?

We check power, rigging and access early and plan around them before the day.

Pricing and booking

What it costs.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scale, crew, show days and the venue. See pricing for starting points, then send your project for a quote.

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form with the space, the dates and what has to happen. The clearer the brief, the faster the quote.

Do you do permanent installs?

Yes: lobbies, showrooms, offices and galleries, including cabling, networks, equipment, training and support after.
