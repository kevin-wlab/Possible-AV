Questions

Experiential production, briefly.

What is experiential production?

Building the physical experience: the interactive pieces, the set, the projection and sound, and the crew that installs and runs it.

Do you design the experience or build what an agency designed?

Both. Some projects come with creative direction from an agency or studio; on others we take it from the first sketch.

Can people interact with the piece?

Yes. We build interactive floors, walls and screens with sensors, body tracking and RFID or NFC triggers.

Can it be tested before the event?

Yes. Pieces are built and tested in Brooklyn before they are installed.

Can the experience travel?

Yes. The same build and crew can go to several cities. See touring.
