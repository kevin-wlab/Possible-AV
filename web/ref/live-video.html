Questions

Live video & visuals, briefly.

Where can you stream to?

To the platform you use. We set up the stream, test it before doors and record it at the same time.

Can we rent a switcher without an operator?

Yes. Switchers and production kits are available with or without an operator, set up and tested by our crew.

Can you make the visuals as well as play them?

Yes. Content made for the show, played live in Resolume and reacting to the sound in the space.

How do remote speakers join?

They join over a call that we bring into the show: on the screens, in the sound system and on the stream.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.
