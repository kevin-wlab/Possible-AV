Questions

Logistics & touring, briefly.

Do you deliver and collect?

Yes. Our own truck and crew deliver, load in, strike and take everything away.

Can you work from the street if there is no dock?

Yes. Everything travels in cases on casters, and comes off the truck at the street or the dock.

Do you tour?

Yes. The same system and the same crew travel with the show to every city on the run.

Where are you based?

In Brooklyn, New York, with our own loading dock. For work outside New York, the same crew travels with the equipment.
