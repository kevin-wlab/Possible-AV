Questions

Short answers.

Do you rent, sell or install?

All three. Most equipment can be rented for an event. Displays, projectors, directional speakers, network equipment and computers can also be bought. We install permanent systems in offices, venues, galleries, retail and homes.

Can we hire only the crew?

Yes. We send audio, video and lighting technicians to shows that already have their equipment.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.

Do you rent LED walls?

Yes, through our LED site, ledwall.nyc.

Can you build something custom?

Yes. We design and fabricate custom pieces and pop-up units with the screens, light and sound built in, and we run large-format 3D printing through ctrlp.studio.

What if we need something that is not listed?

We source it and integrate it with the rest of the system.
