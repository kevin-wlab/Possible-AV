Questions

Sound system rental, briefly.

How big a sound system do I need?

It depends on the space, the number of guests and what is on the program: speech, background music or a live band. Tell us those and we will size the system.

Does a sound system rental come with an engineer?

Yes. We set it up and check it, and an engineer can stay to mix the show.

Can I rent just speakers for a party?

Yes. A pair of speakers with a microphone is a normal job, delivered and set up.

Do you rent directional speakers?

Yes. We rent and sell directional speakers for exhibitions, stores and installs where the sound has to stay in one spot.

Do you install sound systems?

Yes. Background and zoned audio for stores, lobbies, offices and homes, installed and handed over.
