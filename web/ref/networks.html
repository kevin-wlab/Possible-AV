Questions

Networks & computers, briefly.

Why bring our own network when the building has Wi-Fi?

So the event does not depend on it. Your network carries only your event: guests, staff, payments, the stream.

Can you install a permanent network?

Yes. UniFi networks designed, installed and handed over in your space, with support after.

Can we rent computers for the show?

Yes. Workstations and show computers, set up with your software for the run of the event.

Do you build custom PCs?

Yes. Workstations built to order for what they have to run, tested before they ship.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.
