Questions

Audio visual company, briefly.

What does an audio visual company do?

It supplies and runs the screens, sound, lighting and networks for events, and designs and installs AV systems for offices, showrooms and homes. Possible AV does both, in house.

Do you install AV in offices?

Yes: conference rooms, lobbies and showrooms, including displays, audio, control, cabling and the network, with training and support after.

Do you install home theater in New York?

Yes. We supply and install projectors, screens and sound for homes, and set the system up so it is easy to use.

Where are you based?

Brooklyn, New York. We work across the five boroughs, and the same crew travels for work outside the city.

How do we start?

Send the space, the dates and what it has to do through the quote form. We reply within 24 hours.
