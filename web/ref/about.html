Questions

About us, briefly.

What does Possible AV do?

AV rental, installation and production: displays, projection, audio, lighting and staging, live video, networks, interactive, fabrication, and the logistics to bring it all.

Where are you based?

In Brooklyn, New York. For work outside New York, the same crew travels with the equipment.

Do you make content as well as bring equipment?

Yes. Content, 3D and custom builds are made in house, alongside the equipment and the crew.

What if you do not carry something we need?

We source it and bring it in with everything else, on one quote.

Do you have LED walls?

LED walls are handled by our sister site, ledwall.nyc. Large-format 3D printing is at ctrlp.studio.
