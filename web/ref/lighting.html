Lighting & staging · the range

As much rig as the event needs.

We size it to the space and the show, then bring it, build it and run it.

01 · Event lighting

Soft light, shaped on site.

Aputure fixtures for stages, spaces and camera, placed, focused and run by us.

  • Softboxes for faces, barn doors to shape the beam
  • On stands, on truss or flown over the space
  • Placed, focused and run by our crew

02 · Stage decks

A stage that arrives flat.

Modular decks in the size and height the space needs, built and levelled by our crew.

  • Legs go in, decks lock together
  • Levelled on site, with steps and a skirt
  • Struck and taken away after the show

03 · Truss & rigging

Truss, raised into place.

Ground-supported and flown truss for lights, screens, speakers and projectors.

  • Built at working height, then lifted on hoists
  • Ground-supported, or flown from the ceiling
  • Rigged by our own crew

04 · Lighting control

A desk, and someone at it.

A lighting desk and an operator for the show.

  • Looks built and cued for each part of the show
  • An operator at the desk for the whole show
  • Crew-only hire for shows that have their rig

Questions

Lighting & staging, briefly.

Can you light for camera as well as for the audience?

Yes. Faces, stages and products are lit so they look right in the space and on a stream or a recording.

How big a stage can you build?

Decks lock together in the size and height the job needs. Tell us the space and the show and we size it.

Can you hang lights and screens from the ceiling?

Yes, where the building allows it. Otherwise the truss stands on its own towers.

Can we hire only a lighting operator?

Yes. We send lighting technicians and operators to shows that already have their rig.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.
