Questions

Interactive & tracking, briefly.

Do visitors need to wear anything?

No. Cameras and sensors follow people without anything worn. Badges or tagged objects are only needed for RFID and NFC triggers.

What do you build interactive content in?

TouchDesigner, with 3D models and real-time content made in house for the space.

Can you rent a motion tracking system?

Yes. Tracking systems are rented, installed, calibrated and run by our technicians.

Can an installation stay permanently?

Yes. We design and install it for the space, hand it over, and support it after.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.
