Questions

Event lighting rental, briefly.

Do you provide an operator with the lighting?

Yes. A lighting operator can run the desk for the whole event, and the crew sets up and strikes.

Can the lighting work on camera?

Yes. We light panels and talks so they look right in the space and on the stream.

Can you hang lights from truss?

Yes. Truss and rigging go out with the lighting when the space calls for it.

Do you also rent a stage?

Yes. Stage decks, truss and lighting can come together; see stage rental.
