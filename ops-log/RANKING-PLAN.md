# Ranking plan — Possible AV (written 2026-11-23)

Why this exists: the site is weeks old with no outside links. Search Console (2026-08-21 → 11-21) shows the money terms at positions 50–80 and 0 clicks. Results for "AV rental NYC"-type searches are led by vendor directories and long-established rental houses. So the plan is not to fight the head terms first. Audit behind this: `sessions/2026-11-23-geo-crawl-audit.md`.

## 1. Map pack first (fastest: 2–4 weeks) — owner
The three map results above the normal listings are driven by the Google Business Profile, not site authority.
- [ ] Ask 5–10 past clients for Google reviews (largest single factor).
- [ ] Fill the profile: primary category "Audio visual equipment rental service", every service as its own item, real hours, 10+ photos.
- [ ] Post one update a week.
- [ ] (agent) Draft the service items and the first update posts for the owner to paste.

## 2. Borrow the directories' rankings (2–6 weeks) — owner, agent prepares the kit
The directories already own page one, and AI answers cite them.
- [ ] Free listings on the event-vendor marketplaces and local directories that show up for "AV rental NYC" / "AV rental Brooklyn".
- [ ] (agent) One listing kit: company description (short + long), service list, address, email, hours — identical everywhere, matching the footer and JSON-LD.
- [ ] (agent) Add each live listing to `sameAs`.
