Questions

Projection, briefly.

Can you map one picture across several screens?

Yes. Screens and panels of different sizes, with the projectors hung from the ceiling or a truss, and one picture fitted across all of them. Walls, objects, sets and buildings too.

Can we bring our own content?

Yes. We fit your content to the surface, or make content for it.

Do you do home theater?

Yes. A projector, screen and sound at home, rented for a night or installed for good.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.

What if we need something you don't list?

Ask. If we don't carry it, we source it and make it work with the rest of the system.
