Questions

Fabrication, briefly.

Can you build a one-off piece for a single event?

Yes. Housings, plinths, props and mounts are made for the job, and our crew delivers and sets them up.

Does a pop-up unit arrive ready to use?

Yes. It arrives finished, with its display, light and sound already built in.

How large can you print?

Up to furniture scale. Large-format printing and small-batch runs on the print farm go through our print studio, ctrlp.studio.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the build.

What if the piece needs something you don't list?

Ask. We source it and build it into the piece.
