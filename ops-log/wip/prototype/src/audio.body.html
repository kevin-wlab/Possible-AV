02 · Wireless microphones

Handheld, lavalier, headset.

For panels, presenters and performers, with someone watching the channels while they are live.

  • Set up, checked and monitored by us

03 · Directional speakers

Sound that stays in one spot.

The person standing under it hears it. The person next to them does not.

  • For exhibits, retail and shared rooms
  • Hung overhead or mounted on a wall
  • To rent or to buy

04 · Spatial audio & haptic seating

Sound around you. Bass you feel.

Sound placed and moved around the audience, and seating that moves with the low end.

  • Multi-channel sound for immersive rooms
  • Transducers built into the seating
  • Designed with the content, not after it

Questions

Audio, briefly.

Can you send only an audio engineer?

Yes. We send engineers to shows that already have their equipment, and we run our own systems when we supply them.

Do you sell directional speakers, or only rent them?

Both. They can be rented for an event or bought for a permanent install.

Do you install sound permanently?

Yes, for venues, galleries, retail, conference rooms and homes, including the network it runs on.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We are based in Brooklyn, New York, and the same crew travels with the equipment.

Do you do silent disco or interpretation headsets?

No. We also don't provide power distribution.
