Numbered tall cards 01/02 (engagement models), testimonials, "two ways to work with us". Fixed top-right Menu + "Book a call" pills. Fonts: Roobert + Orbikular. Light mint-grey ground.

WE PREFER CLARITY." with floating 3D shapes, outlined marquee "DESIGN · DEVELOP · WIX · SHIP", process steps, skills, numbered service cards (01–06) with pill tags, "PARTNER. LAUNCH FAST."
