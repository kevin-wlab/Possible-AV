- Type: Geist Sans; headlines 24px/600/-0.84px; body 15px; hierarchy by weight + grey (#EDEDED / #A1A1A1 / #6F6F6F). One accent #52A8FF.
- Motion: staggered rise-in, sliding nav pill, link previews, spring dock. No preloader/scroll-jacking.

- Don't: 24px headlines, imageless 704px column, résumé structure.
